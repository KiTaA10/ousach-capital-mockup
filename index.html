02 Our approach

How we work.

We love to be among the earliest investors. We make our investment decisions fast and keep the process straightforward.

  1. 01

    Clear, quick decisions

    We ask direct questions and give founders a clear answer as early as we can.

  2. 02

    Flexible investing

    We typically invest alongside a lead and can follow on selectively as the company grows.

  3. 03

    Help only when we actually can

    Where our experience or network can help, we get involved. Otherwise, founders retain the space to build.
